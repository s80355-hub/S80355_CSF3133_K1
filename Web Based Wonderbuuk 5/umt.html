About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as the Fisheries and Marine Science centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in 1979, providing facilities for practical training and research for students in fisheries and marine science program.

In May 1999, the Cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) as an associate campus of UPM, which was later given autonomy in 2001 and renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM). On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was officially elevated to a full-fledged university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT stands as Malaysia's primier university focused on marine science, aquatic resources and maritime studies, driving impactful research and sustainable development for coastal dan global communities.
